**Title:** The Bus Doesn't Think. It Carries Things.

**Short description:** Ideas pass through people, tools and models. Follow the cargo to ask what changed, who contributed, who is accountable and how a human can leave.

## Abstract

An idea can pass from one person through a model to another person and arrive sounding as though it began at the last stop. This talk follows that journey. The bus is a carrier; its cargo is language, jokes, beliefs and credit. Using a conductor, a hidden credit list and examples of ideas changing costume, we distinguish carrying, editing, originating and endorsing. The same symbol earns another appearance only when it does a different job. Along the way, three practical questions remain with the human: where did this come from, who answers for what happens next, and can I walk away? This is a talk about transmission, collaboration and provenance, with room for disagreement.

## Attendees will learn to

- Distinguish a source from its rendering, retelling or remix.
- Describe separate contributions without crediting the courier with every part of the cargo.
- Apply provenance, accountability and exit questions to a model-assisted collaboration.

**Audience:** Developers, open-source contributors, community organizers and anyone collaborating with AI; no model-training background required.
